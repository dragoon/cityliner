# Credits and marks

Gelasio Regular is bundled under [SIL OFL 1.1](../assets/fonts/Gelasio-OFL.txt); [source URLs and hashes](../assets/fonts/Gelasio-SOURCE.json) identify the TTF and WOFF2. Custom fonts are optional.
Date-aware posters show peak hourly scheduled service for the selected week. Titles and credits overlay the map; unreliable geometry is excluded.
Generated PDFs and palette variants belong in ignored `posters/`; `gallery/` keeps only README examples.

## Data

| Example | Source and terms |
| --- | --- |
| Berlin | [VBB](https://unternehmen.vbb.de/gtfs), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Helsinki | [HSL](https://www.hsl.fi/en/hsl/open-data), CC BY 4.0; credit HSL and delivery date |
| Tallinn | [City timetable](https://transport.tallinn.ee/); no CC licence assumed |
| Zürich | [SBB / SKI](https://opentransportdata.swiss/en/terms-of-use/); cite `opentransportdata.swiss` |

The examples use 14–20 October 2026 schedules; Zürich uses national SBB version 20261010 and validated OSM geometry, across all modes, without ZVV shapes.
Water and Swiss route geometry require [OSM attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), including `openstreetmap.org/copyright` and ODbL. Preserve CC BY provider, date, modification and licence information.
Water dates differ from timetable dates: Berlin/Helsinki/Tallinn inland water is from 13 October, Zürich from 8 October; Helsinki/Tallinn coastlines use an older cache. Detailed provenance stays in each bundle and the website.

## Logos and coats of arms

Logo assets are preserved but omitted by default. Dataset licences do not grant trademark rights; use marks only where entitled. A disclaimer does not grant permission.
[Berlin](https://www.berlin.de/sen/inneres/buerger-und-staat/verfassungs-und-verwaltungsrecht/hoheitszeichen/landeswappen/artikel.24365.php) permits respectful artistic depictions of its arms. [Helsinki](https://www.hel.fi/en/decision-making/information-on-helsinki/design-and-digitalisation/helsinki-brand-and-visual-identity/visual-identity-guidelines/use-basic-elements) permits specified private uses under heraldic guidelines; its framed logo requires separate permission.
[Tallinn](https://www.tallinn.ee/en/trademarks-and-symbols-city) specifies written authorisation. Zürich follows [Swiss CAPA Article 8](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/23383): ordinary decorative posters are not clearly within its exceptions; an applicable exception or city authorisation is needed.
Existing Berlin and Helsinki SVGs are branded logos, not their traditional arms. Logos retain their own rights and are not covered by the code licence.
