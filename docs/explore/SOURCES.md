# Demo data

The public explorer demo uses a fictional network generated by
`scripts/build_explorer_demo.py`. Its dates are 1–7 January 2024 in UTC.
It contains no real city, agency timetable, or water extract.

For your own published bundles, retain agency and converter credits, GTFS
terms, water provenance, coverage dates, and the processing report. Follow
[README.md](README.md) for export and staging commands.
