The method

Daily rhythm uses fixed weekly peaks, keeping rare service subdued. Departures shows absolute intensity. Change from 08:00 highlights increases and decreases on the selected date. Playback blends adjacent samples; pause to read the source timetable counts.

Frequency-based services show expected departures. These are schedules, not live vehicle positions. Faint lines provide network context.
